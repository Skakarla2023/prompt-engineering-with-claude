## Discernment

- It refers to the ability to critically evaluate what AI produces, how it behaves and how it produces the result.
- For that you need:
  - domain expertise
  - an understanding of how AI systems work and their short comings.
- It is done in 3 steps:
  - Product Discernment
  - Process Discernment
  - 

### Product Discernment

- It is the ability to judge the quality of the AI output.
- Asking yourself questions like:
  - Factually accurate?
  - Appropriate to target audience?
  - well-strctured?
  - Does it meet my requirements?
  - Does it add value?
 

### Process Discernment

- It is the capacity to judge the quality of the problem solving approach.
- Look for things like:
  - logical consistency
  - lapses in attention
  - inappropriate steps
  - getting stuck on one small detail
  - getting trapped in circular reasoning


### Performance Discernment

- Your ability to evaluate and guide AI behavior during your interaction.
- Ask yourself questions like this:
  - Is the communication style appropriate?
  - Is the information at the right level?
  - Is response to feedback appropriate?
  - Is the interaction efficient?

### Feedback Correction

- Effective feedback includes:
  - Specifying the problem
  - Clearly explaining why it is a problem
  - Providing concrete suggestions for improvement
  - Revising your instructions or examples





- **Product Discernment**: Evaluating the quality of AI outputs
- **Process Discernment**: Assessing how the AI approached the task
- **Performance Discernment**: Evaluating how the AI behaved during the interaction itself
