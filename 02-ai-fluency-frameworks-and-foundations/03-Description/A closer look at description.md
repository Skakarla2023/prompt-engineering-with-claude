## Description

- It is the task where you communicate with the **AI**.
- It needs the bility to clearly define the characteristics of your desired output.
- Give the AI the information it needs to deliver what you're actually looking for, not just assuming what you want.
- It includes context, format, target auidence, style and other constraints.
- Don't let the AI assume and do its work, instead provide it with clear instructions of how you want the output to be, with explicit instructions.
- It involves three stages:
  - Product Description
  - Process Description
  - Performance Description
 

### Product Description

- It is the ability to clearly define the characteristics of your desired output.
- It is the ability of defining the AI what you want.
- Instead of simply giving vague prompts where you dont clearly describe what you want, provide the ai with the contect, format and style .
- Give the AI the information it needs to deliver what you're actually looking for, but not what it assumes.


### Process Description

- It refers to the ability to guide the AI's thought process.
- Sometimes asking "How" is important than "What".
- providing the AI with the training specific to your problem, given its already trained model.
- Mentioning specific data, key tasks and preferred order of tasks.
- It refers the ability to guide how the AI assistant approaches your request.


### Performance Description

- The ability to define the behavioral aspects of an AI interaction.
- AI tools are interactive systems that can be behave differently in different contexts.
- You need to expain how you want the AI to behave to get the best results.



**Note**: By nurturing your capacity for product, process and performance **description**, you can tranform AI tools from **generic assistants** into **finely tuned thinking partners**.
